# Image3DConversion Login Portal

## Operations Head Review: Service Positioning, User Engagement and Partner Revenue

**Review status:** Business and operations recommendation  
**Development boundary:** No redesign of the approved interface, navigation, design tokens, security rules, role structure, tenant isolation, database architecture or current case-submission workflow.

---

## 1. Executive Direction

The portal should evolve from a secure case-upload environment into the **digital treatment operating environment for a dental practice**.

It should not look or behave like:

- an online dental shop;
- a crowded laboratory catalogue;
- a sponsor advertising wall;
- a directory with unverified specialists; or
- a second SurgiGuideExcellence education platform.

The strongest commercial position is:

> **Image3DConversion helps a practice plan, prepare, execute and complete digital treatment—with the right design, specialist, laboratory and technology partner connected at the right stage.**

### Non-negotiable hierarchy

1. **Hero Treatment Solution — Guided Implant Surgery**
2. **Other Digital Treatment Solutions**
3. **Clinical Expert and Prosthetic Support**
4. **Practice Technology and Affiliate Partner Solutions**
5. **Learning, case preparation and contextual help**

Guided Implant Surgery must remain the largest, first and most visually dominant solution. Partner products and other services should strengthen this workflow, not compete with it.

### Recommended portal descriptor

Keep the Image3DConversion name. Use **Digital Treatment Hub** as a positioning descriptor, not as an immediate rebrand:

> **Image3DConversion Digital Treatment Hub**  
> Plan. Prepare. Execute. Complete.

Until specialist scheduling and partner transactions are operational, use **Request**, **Connect** or **Get Matched**—not **Book Now**, **Buy Now** or **Marketplace**.

## 2. Proposed Service Architecture

### A. Guided Implant Surgery — Hero

- Single and multiple implant planning
- Surgical guides
- Full-arch and stackable workflows
- FP1, FP2 and FP3 pathways
- Immediate loading
- Zygoma and pterygoid planning
- Surgery-support coordination
- Temporary-to-final prosthetic completion

### B. Digital Treatment Solutions

| Solution family | Services to position |
|---|---|
| **Implant & Full-Arch** | Planning, guides, stackable guides, immediate loading, FP1/FP2/FP3, zygoma, pterygoid |
| **Smile & Periodontal** | Smile design, restorative planning, virtual wax-up, gingivectomy/crown-lengthening guides, immediate temporaries |
| **Endodontic** | Guided endodontic access, apicoectomy planning/guides, imaging support |
| **Orthodontic** | Clear-aligner planning and digitally coordinated orthodontic treatment |
| **Digital Prosthetics** | Crowns and bridges, implant prosthetics, immediate provisionals, full-arch provisional-to-final, digital dentures |
| **OMFS & Complex Care** | Complex-case discussion, maxillofacial planning, specialist coordination and applicable surgical-guide workflows |
| **Imaging & Diagnostics** | DICOM-to-STL, segmentation, implant radiology, TMJ reporting, pathology reporting and related diagnostic services |

### C. Four fulfilment modes

These four labels should be used consistently across the portal so customers understand the level of support without reading a technical catalogue:

1. **Design Only** — approved digital design files for the practice or its production partner.
2. **Design to Print** — design plus coordinated printable/manufactured output.
3. **Ready to Treat** — the required planning, guide or prosthetic component prepared for chairside execution.
4. **Temporary to Final** — coordinated provisional and definitive prosthetic workflow.

For complex procedures, a fifth optional service layer can be presented:

5. **Expert Supported** — an appropriately credentialed clinical expert is requested to support or lead execution.

### D. Practice Enablement

This is the affiliate layer. It should be titled **Practice Technology** or **Partner Solutions**, not Shop.

| Category | Affiliate opportunities |
|---|---|
| **Imaging & Capture** | CBCT, IOS, face scanners, photography systems, photogrammetry |
| **Implant Execution** | Implant systems, scan bodies, guided kits, conventional surgical kits, full-arch components |
| **Chairside Production** | 3D printers, resins, washing and curing systems, milling and finishing solutions |
| **Digital Workflow** | Planning/CAD tools, workstations and clinically relevant workflow software |
| **Fulfilment & Support** | Printing, milling, laboratory support, equipment onboarding, calibration and application support |

Image3DConversion should not own stock or become responsible for fulfilment. It should qualify the practice need, recommend an appropriate solution, introduce the affiliate partner and track the commercial outcome.

### E. Expert and Fulfilment Networks

Two distinct networks are required:

- **Clinical Expert Network:** implant surgery, orthodontics, OMFS, prosthodontics, endodontics and smile-restoration support.
- **Prosthetic Partner Network:** laboratories and production partners that can accept prosthetic work—with or without an Image3DConversion guided solution.

In the present portal these should begin as **concierge-managed requests**, not open marketplaces.

## 3. Experience and Design Direction

### Dashboard hierarchy

| Visual priority | Portal content |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Start a Guided Implant Case, active cases, clinical actions and case status |
| **Secondary** | Explore Digital Treatment Solutions, Request Clinical Expert, Complete the Prosthetic Workflow |
| **Supporting** | Practice Technology, Learn Centre, Partner Spotlight and announcements |

The Guided Implant Surgery action should occupy approximately twice the visual weight of any other individual solution card. Other treatment services can appear in a clean grid below it. Affiliate placements should remain below clinical work and never enter immersive upload, review or active case-communication screens.

### Card design

Every service card should answer four questions:

1. What treatment outcome does this support?
2. When should the practice use it?
3. What must the practice upload or prepare?
4. What will Image3DConversion coordinate or deliver?

Use:

- approved blue palette, white and neutral surfaces;
- Inter typography;
- consistent line icons and clinically relevant imagery;
- short outcome-led titles;
- one clear action per card;
- restrained badges such as **Hero Solution**, **Expert Support Available**, **Partner Fulfilled** or **Featured Partner**.

Do not add a third brand colour solely for affiliate visibility. Partner identity can appear inside the product/detail card while the portal shell remains Image3DConversion.

### Commercial placement rules

- No promotions inside file upload, case review, approval, clinical communication or surgery-day workflows.
- Show products only when relevant to a treatment or practice need.
- Label paid or sponsored visibility clearly.
- Display fulfilment, warranty and after-sales ownership as the affiliate partner’s responsibility.
- Avoid discount-led banners. Lead with clinical compatibility, workflow improvement and application support.
- Permit only one primary Partner Spotlight at a time on the dashboard.
- Keep SurgiGuideExcellence event marketing outside the service portal. The Learn Centre may contain product manuals, scan protocols and workflow guidance, but not become an education-event feed.

### Immediate operating model

Until dedicated backend tracking exists:

1. Customer selects **Request Clinical Expert**, **Match My Practice Technology** or **Complete This Treatment**.
2. The request enters an existing authorised help or case-communication channel.
3. Operations qualifies the requirement and obtains consent to introduce a partner.
4. Operations assigns the approved partner outside the portal.
5. The outcome and commercial amount are recorded in the company’s authorised CRM/finance process.
6. The portal never represents an introduction as confirmed until operations confirms it.

This is suitable for demand validation, but it is not a scalable substitute for referral tracking.

## 7. Affiliate Business Model Without Inventory

Image3DConversion should operate as a **workflow-led demand generator and treatment orchestrator**.

### Commercial models

| Model | How it works | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualified introduction fee** | Partner pays for an accepted, criteria-matched practice introduction | Equipment demonstrations and high-value systems |
| **Closed-sale commission** | I3DC receives an agreed percentage or fixed margin after a completed sale | CBCT, IOS, printers, photogrammetry and larger systems |
| **Workflow bundle margin** | Partner product is included in a defined treatment/practice package | Implant systems, kits, scan bodies, resins and prosthetic fulfilment |
| **Fulfilment margin** | I3DC routes a service to a lab/production partner and retains an agreed margin | Printing, milling, provisionals and final prosthetics |
| **Sponsored clinical placement** | Partner funds a clearly labelled featured solution or workflow guide | Launches, campaigns and event-linked partners |
| **Application-support package** | I3DC is compensated for onboarding a practice into a clinically usable workflow | Scanner, printer, planning software and guided-kit adoption |

### What makes the I3DC lead valuable

I3DC should not send partners an unqualified “interested customer.” The request should include:

- practice location;
- treatment goal;
- current equipment/capabilities;
- expected case volume;
- product or workflow gap;
- requested timeline;
- permission to introduce the partner; and
- whether clinical application support is required.

This is the difference between a generic affiliate click and a high-value, workflow-qualified opportunity.

### Partner governance required

- written margin/referral terms;
- accepted lead definition;
- attribution period;
- geographic/service coverage;
- named fulfilment and after-sales owner;
- sponsor disclosure;
- clinical-claims approval;
- response SLA;
- customer-consent standard;
- complaint and escalation process; and
- monthly reconciliation.

## 8. Recommended Customer Journeys

### Journey 1 — Guided surgery

**Start Guided Case → select clinical workflow → choose fulfilment mode → upload → review → request expert support if needed → plan/guide → temporary/final prosthetic completion**

This is the hero journey and should receive the strongest design treatment.

### Journey 2 — Other digital treatment

**Explore Treatment Solutions → choose treatment outcome → see records required → select fulfilment mode → begin through the existing case route or request concierge support**

### Journey 3 — Practice technology

**Practice Readiness → identify workflow gap → Match My Practice Technology → operations qualifies → approved partner introduction → partner fulfils → I3DC tracks outcome and margin**

### Journey 4 — Specialist-supported treatment

**Request Clinical Expert → specialty and treatment need → records/solution requirement → operations validates → expert proposed → customer confirms → treatment coordinated**

Until the future booking architecture exists, the portal must display **Requested**, **Under Review**, **Expert Proposed** and **Confirmed** truthfully. It must not imply that submitting a request secures a specialist.

### Journey 5 — Prosthetic partner fulfilment

**Planning/case stage → Complete the Prosthetic Workflow → operations qualifies scope → approved lab introduced/assigned → lab fulfils → I3DC captures margin**

The first version remains concierge-managed. The future lab workspace can add acceptance, file exchange, milestones and financial settlement.

## 9. Operational Readiness Before Commercial Launch

The portal should not expose a service or partner category until the business has:

1. a named internal owner;
2. a customer-facing scope and exclusion statement;
3. a fulfilment partner or internal delivery owner;
4. defined input requirements;
5. expected turnaround or response SLA;
6. a pricing, referral or margin rule;
7. consent and data-sharing rules;
8. an escalation path; and
9. a truthful portal status vocabulary.

### Minimum partner onboarding record

- legal/business name;
- category and brands;
- service geography;
- named relationship owner;
- product catalogue approved by I3DC;
- clinical/application claims approved;
- lead-response SLA;
- fulfilment, warranty and after-sales responsibility;
- commercial terms;
- sponsored-placement status; and
- active/inactive approval state.

This record may be maintained operationally outside the portal initially. Partner self-service should wait for the future Partner Studio.

## 10. Commercial and Experience Measures

Track the following before building a large marketplace:

- percentage of customers who open Treatment Solutions;
- Guided Implant Surgery start-to-submit conversion;
- incomplete submission rate after using Case Readiness;
- requests for expert support by specialty and geography;
- prosthetic attachment rate per eligible case;
- product-match requests by category;
- partner acceptance and response time;
- qualified introduction-to-sale conversion;
- affiliate revenue and gross margin by partner/category;
- repeat-case rate at 30, 60 and 90 days;
- number of practices using two or more I3DC service families; and
- support resolution time and customer retention.

These measures will show which services deserve backend investment and which should remain concierge-led.

## Final Operations Recommendation

Do not begin by building a marketplace.

Begin by making Image3DConversion’s full treatment capability discoverable inside the approved portal, then add three tightly controlled concierge actions:

1. **Request Clinical Expert**
2. **Complete the Prosthetic Workflow**
3. **Match My Practice Technology**

These actions let the business test specialist demand, lab routing and affiliate product revenue immediately without weakening Guided Implant Surgery, redesigning the portal or giving outside partners unsafe access.

Once the requests produce repeatable volume, measurable margin and dependable fulfilment, the data will justify the Partner Studio, specialist booking, lab workspace and commission architecture. This sequence protects the current development while moving Image3DConversion toward a commercially viable, industry-wide digital treatment ecosystem.
